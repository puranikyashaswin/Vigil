# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

A training methodology used for models like InstructGPT and ChatGPT. First fine-tunes a base model on a small dataset of prompts and desired outputs, then trains a reward model by having human labelers rank outputs, and finally applies reinforcement learning (e.g., Proximal Policy Optimization) to update the LLM weights based on the reward model.
