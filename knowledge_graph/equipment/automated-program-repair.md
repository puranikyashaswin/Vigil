# Automated Program Repair

A research area focused on automatically generating patches for buggy programs. Techniques include template-based, heuristic-based, constraint-based, NMT-based, and LLM-based approaches. Tools operate under the Generate and Validate paradigm of producing candidate patches and validating them against test suites.
