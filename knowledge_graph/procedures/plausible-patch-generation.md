# Plausible Patch Generation

A step in ChatRepair that occurs after a plausible patch is found. ChatRepair prompts ChatGPT with the initial prompt plus the list of previously generated plausible patches and instructs it to generate alternative variations, increasing the chance of producing a correct patch. On average adds 9.4, 16.6, and 5.5 additional plausible patches in single-line, single-hunk, and single-function scenarios respectively on Defects4j 1.2.
